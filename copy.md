## SLIDE 6 — THREE TOOLS FOR YOUR PRACTICE

**Headline:**
> Three Tools. Built for Ortho Surgeons.
> No New Hardware. No IT Team. Ready in Weeks.

---

**Tool 1 — The Consent Guardian**
*Medico-legal protection that actually holds*

Patient watches a short video of you explaining the procedure.
Answers 3 comprehension questions.
Timestamp, name, and correct answers are permanently logged.

> "From signature to verified understanding."

The next time a patient says "I didn't know the risks" — you have documented proof that they did.

*No new workflow. Runs on a tablet in your waiting room.*

---

**Tool 2 — Smart Discharge Voice Bot**
*Your instructions, in their language, at the right time*

Your discharge summary → personalised 60-second audio message → sent via WhatsApp → in the patient's own language. Hindi, Punjabi, and more.

> "Your bedside manner, in their pocket."

Fewer 11 PM phone calls. Higher patient satisfaction. Better post-op adherence.

*Runs entirely on WhatsApp. Your patient already has it.*

---

**Tool 3 — Pre-Op Implant Sizing Assistant**
*Know your implant before you make the first cut*

AI analysis of your standard 2D X-rays to suggest implant sizes and alignment angles — based on published landmark studies from Gleamer, OrthoGrid, and peer-reviewed literature.

> "Accuracy comparable to manual templating — at a fraction of the time."

Reduced intraoperative adjustment time. Less implant inventory guesswork. Fewer surprises.

## COPY NOTES

- Slide 1 opens with a story, not a tech statement — the audience doesn't know it's an AI talk until 30 seconds in
- "Mako and ROSA" named in Slide 2 signals this was built for ortho surgeons specifically, not repurposed from a generic AI conference
- Slide 3 is aspirational, not corrective — no "you're doing it wrong" framing
- Slide 6 lead line ("No new hardware. No IT team.") preempts the two biggest objections before they form
- Tool 3 accuracy claim is grounded in "published studies" not a standalone percentage — defensible if challenged
- Slide 6 close uses scarcity framing ("two or three surgeons") — partnership, not sales pitch
- Slide 7 ask is time-bound and physically specific ("next 15 minutes," "walk up") — proven to increase in-person follow-through
- QR code should link to WhatsApp direct message, not just a website
- Consider a physical card: three tools on one side, WhatsApp on the other
